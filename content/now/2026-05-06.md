## 🧑‍💻 Work

- We officially renamed from MaestroQA to [Rippit][https://rippit.com] as a move to re-scope the product to be about conversation analytics more generally rather than just about conversations with support or other customer quality assurance. It has felt like we are in experiment and prototype mode, so there have been some high-effort weeks, but it feels more like a team environment than any time I have been here.
- Trips to New York are fun every couple months, but I definitely have to recover from them. It is fun to be back into 20 year old mode where I can spend all day thinking about something I am passionate about without distractions of home life, but coming home and being around the people I love is the norm I prefer, even if the hyper-focus is a nice change of pace.

## 🧠 Thinking About

- Gratitude. There are so many times that I have been seeing, where I could either look at a situation to see the bad part of it or the good part, and being able to consciously choose to have the more generous feeling has made accepting things outside of my control so much easier. When things are bad, rather than focus on that, I have been thinking of all the conditions that exist to make that bad thing feel bad, when if I were in a worse place, I wouldn't have the opportunity to feel it since so many other things would be top of mind.
