## 🧑‍💻 Work

- After a tumultuous time at Rippit, I’m starting at [SeatGeek](https://seatgeek.com/) in November! Rippit was leaning hard into a moon-shot prototype culture, and I am really looking forward to working closely with other engineers on customer-centric goals while still being centered on how best to use artificial intelligence to support that.
- The people I talked to during interviews there were incredibly kind and focused on how to lean on each other and ensure that they take the right moves while moving faster than traditional product planning allows. It should be a great adventure!

## 🍿 Media Consumption

- For the most part, my reading has remained lighter books, but the [biography of John Marshall Harlan](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/55710575-the-great-dissenter) was incredible. The history of the Supreme Court is fascinating to me in general, but the life story of this man who set the stage for twentieth-century jurisprudence was astounding because of how much his perceptions evolved, as well as how integrated they were into his decisions.
- To ensure that I got to see the film “as Christopher Nolan intended it”, I went on a 12 hour road trip to Indianapolis to see [The Odyssey](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt33764258) in 70mm IMAX and it absolutely held up. Part of the fun was the journey involved, and I am very sure it would be great in any form factor, but it was a fun trip regardless.
- As always, movies are a big part of my life, but the recent obsession has been [The Diplomat](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt17491088), which I found through its companion podcast [The West Wing Weekly](https://thewestwingweekly.com/), where they are analyzing every episode as a “book club” of sorts. Neem and I are binging it together, and I had forgotten how much fun that experience is.

## 🎨 Creation

- Video-game development has been on my mind quite a bit, and I spent nearly three months working on a prototype game called [Wizard Tower Builder](https://github.com/katerberg/wizard-tower-builder/), which ended up getting both too complex, and not fun enough to justify. It was an admirable effort and I learned a lot about how to scale a personal project architecture in the world of AI-enhanced scope-creep, but I am quite happy to kill it.
- Dot-Man on the other hand, is a game that I have a lot of hope for. It is a tribute to Pac-Man that has some genuine fun while I am play-testing it, and I am hoping to get it to a spot where it is worth demoing on Steam and sharing more broadly in the next month. If anyone is interested in play-testing the game before then, please let me know! I would love to get more eyes on it and critical feedback.

## 🧠 Thinking About

- Perfectionism has been haunting me as I work on projects. I really love watching Neem build things with friends since she is so fast to get a working version going and then iterating without fear of looking foolish. Between a [book on the founders’ definition of happiness](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/176443822-the-pursuit-of-happiness) and one on [The Gifts of Imperfection](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/7015403-the-gifts-of-imperfection), I am trying to excise the fear of failure by recognizing it as a self-sabotage rather than the drive to quality that I sometimes think of it as. At the end of the day, half-finished projects that I do not release make me feel way worse than finished imperfect projects.
